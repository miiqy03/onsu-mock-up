OUR CHEF

Michael Kwan

Award-winning pastry chef Michael Kwan started his career in the kitchens of Hong Kong and has since gone on to become the Executive Pastry Chef at The Dorchester.

Along the way, he's worked at Hotel Cafe Royal, Ladurée, Hakkasan and The Fat Duck.

Now he's here at Onsu, blending precision with creativity to make you smile every day — perfectly balancing art and science, one ounce of joy at a time.
